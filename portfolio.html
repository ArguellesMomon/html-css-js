Vindicta Movie Details
"Vindicta" is a short psychological thriller we created back in senior high school. It follows the dark and chilling story of a once-silent student who, after years of relentless bullying, takes justice into his own hands — eliminating his tormentors one by one. The film explores themes of revenge, trauma, and the dangerous consequences of unchecked cruelty.
TravelBud App Details
The concept was a social travel companion app that connects solo travelers, groups, and locals exploring Batangas. I focused on creating a clean, user-friendly interface that highlights features like trip matching, local guides, accessible itineraries, and safety verification. The design process involved user research, wireframing, and prototyping that are all centered on making travel more enjoyable, safer, and socially engaging. It was a great opportunity to combine creativity with real-world problem-solving through design.
JeepneyGo App Details
"JeepneyGo" is a mobile app proposal we worked on that aimed to modernize the jeepney commuting experience in the Philippines. Designed for easier daily travel, the app featured real-time tracking, estimated arrival times, route planning, and fare info all tailored to the local commuting culture. The goal was to improve convenience and safety for passengers while supporting local drivers through better route visibility. This project allowed me to explore tech-based solutions for everyday problems and highlighted the importance of user-centered design in public transport.
